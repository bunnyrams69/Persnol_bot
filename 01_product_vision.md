# Life OS - Product Vision

## Vision

Life OS is an AI Chief of Staff for students, freelancers and creators.
It is not a todo list, calendar, or habit tracker.

### Core Promise

-   Plan tomorrow automatically.
-   Follow up throughout the day.
-   Learn habits and patterns.
-   Hold the user accountable.
-   Negotiate tomorrow's plan.

### User Profile

-   College: 9:00 AM -- 3:15 PM (Mon--Fri)
-   Priorities:
    1.  Client deadlines
    2.  Gym (never skip)
    3.  AI Agency
    4.  DSA

### Personality

Acts like an executive coach: - Direct - Intelligent - Slightly blunt -
Never rude - No fake motivation

### End Goal

"I don't need to manage my life. Life OS manages it with me."
